## Direction exploration record

Audience scene: a founder or design-aware client evaluating whether one engineer can connect product experience, application systems, and applied AI. The website must give them both an arresting impression and inspectable work. The familiar hero-plus-card-grid and its terminal/neon opposite were excluded.

Seven grounded systems considered: 1) collectible design monograph; 2) architectural model studio; 3) optical laboratory; 4) musical score; 5) contemporary kinetic exhibition; 6) technical field journal; 7) film-title and contact-sheet sequence. These span publishing, fabrication, instruments, performance, exhibition, and cinema. A direction seed selected the fifth. The proposed exhibition direction uses spatial artifacts, quiet gallery labels, a chapter sequence, and contextual project rooms; it does not pretend Amey is an architect, musician, or gallery institution.

The tool's additional reference systems were evaluated on audience identification and product clarity. None displaced the exhibition direction:

| Alternate system | Audience identification | Product clarity | Verdict and discipline retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Star atlas | Astronomy would become the first association | Geographic coordinates do not explain a product contribution | Declined; retain consistent scale and hierarchy across chapters. |
| Endurance garage | Motorsport identity would overwhelm Amey's actual work | Lap/stint terminology makes engagements harder to read | Declined; retain a continuous identifying element through navigation and transitions. |
| Hand-drawn technical zine | Teaching-zine personality weakens the requested luxurious presentation | Comic panels need more decoding than the direct project narratives here | Declined; retain concise, annotated explanations of hard concepts. |
| Drum-machine sequencer | Music production is not supported by the supplied projects | Sixteen-step sequencing obscures project and contact navigation | Declined; retain precise rhythm and immediate feedback from controls. |
| Chromatophore skin | Biological spectacle would become the subject | Pigment states obscure the interface/system/AI relationship | Declined; retain a meaningful response to the visitor's selected context. |
| Layered paper roundel | Folkloric ornament is distant from the requested professional engineering identity | Radial layer navigation complicates case-study reading | Declined; retain clearly explained depth and separation of layers. |

The actual chosen reference bar is the original visual proposal and the supplied live portfolios. Alternate catalog artwork is labeled inspiration on the decision page, never a promise of the final interface. The exact seed output is preserved separately in the workspace at tmp/design-v3-seed.txt.
